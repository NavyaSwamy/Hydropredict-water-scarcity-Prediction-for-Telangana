## 🌍 Why HydroPredict?

Water scarcity is a growing environmental challenge influenced by:

- 🌧️ Irregular rainfall
- 💧 Declining groundwater levels
- 🌡️ Increasing temperatures
- 📉 Changing water availability
- 🗺️ Regional differences between districts

HydroPredict AI transforms these environmental indicators into an **easy-to-understand district-level water risk intelligence platform**.

Instead of only looking at historical data, the platform brings together:

> **Historical Data → Machine Learning → Risk Classification → Forecasting → Actionable Insights**

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional water-resource analysis often requires examining multiple environmental datasets separately.

This makes it difficult to quickly understand:

- Which districts are experiencing higher water stress?
- How is groundwater changing over time?
- How are rainfall and temperature affecting water availability?
- What could future water-risk conditions look like?
- What actions could be considered for high-risk regions?

**HydroPredict AI addresses these challenges through a unified machine-learning and visualization platform focused on Telangana.**
